## Central plant - multiple

**Report reference:** FSR-3011  
**Visit date:** 10 March 2026

### Summary

Attended for scheduled annual inspection across the central plant. Started at chiller CH-01: checked refrigerant charge, superheat 5K, subcool 4K, both within spec, no action. Moved to CH-02: contactor CC-1 showed pitting on contacts, replaced. CH-03: contactor CC-2 similar condition, replaced as preventive. Boiler BLR-01: combustion analysis showed CO at 42ppm, within limit, no action required beyond routine clean. BLR-02: temperature sensor TS-8 reading 4 degrees high against calibrated reference, replaced. BLR-03: sensor TS-9 open circuit, replaced, verified reading. Cooling tower CT-01: basin cleaned, filter FD-30 replaced, water treatment dosing confirmed operational. AHU-01 through AHU-06: filters checked, all within service interval. Gasket GK-4 on AHU-03 access panel perished, replaced to restore seal. Control panel: relay RL-7 intermittent on test, replaced. Left site with all plant operational. Full plant inspection and multiple remedial actions across six assets.
